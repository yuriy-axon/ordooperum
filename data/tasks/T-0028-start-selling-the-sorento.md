## Description

Start selling the old Kia Sorento (second generation, about 15 years old) in Ukraine. This is critical. Done when the car is on sale, for example listed with an asking price.

## Additional information

- Price: On AUTO.RIA, 2011 Kia Sorento cars are listed at about 10,500–14,700 USD (about 470,000–660,000 UAH), mostly with 190,000–250,000 km. Prices are indicative and depend on mileage, condition, engine and gearbox; compare listings like yours before setting the asking price. — [source](https://auto.ria.com/auto_kia_sorento_40145589.html), 2026-10-28
- Research: The sale is registered at a Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVS) service centre: first an inspection by the MVS Expert Service, then buyer and seller visit a service centre together with passport or ID card, tax ID number and the vehicle registration certificate; re-registration takes about 30 minutes. The price in the sale contract can't be lower than the car's official market value, which the Expert Service can determine. — [source](https://hsc.gov.ua/2026/07/14/prodazh-transportnogo-zasobu-yak-diznatisya-rinkovu-vartist-ta-uspishno-perereyestruvati-avto/), 2026-10-28
- Research: For an individual, the first sale of a passenger car in a calendar year is free of personal income tax, and no annual declaration is needed if there is no other income that must be declared. A second sale in the same year is taxed at 5% plus a 5% military levy, a third and later at 18%. — [source](https://buhplatforma.com.ua/news/124408-prodazh-avto-u-2026-rotsi-koly-splachuvaty-pdfo-vz-ta-podavaty-deklaratsiiu), 2026-10-28
