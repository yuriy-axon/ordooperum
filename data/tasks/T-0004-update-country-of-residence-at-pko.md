## Description

PKO Bank Polski has accepted the explanation that YURII KRAVCHENKO's correct country of residence is Ukraine and that the data in the Central Register of Beneficial Owners (CRBR) is correct. The bank now asks for the country of residence in its own records to be updated to Ukraine. The bank offers three ways to do this: (1) an instruction with the date, full name, the data to update and the correct data, signed with a qualified electronic signature and emailed as an attachment to Informacje@pkobp.pl; (2) a handwritten signed statement with the same content, sent by post, with the signature certified by a Polish notary, a Polish diplomatic mission or consulate, or a foreign notary with an apostille (documents in a foreign language must be translated into Polish by a sworn translator or a consul); or (3) a visit to any PKO Bank Polski branch if neither of the first two is possible. Done when the bank's records show Ukraine as the country of residence.
