## Description

A sales manager sent an email to a contact person, but the company's owner in Pipedrive does not see that email. Find out why the email is not visible to the owner and fix the cause. Done when the owner can see the email in Pipedrive, or the reason it can't be shown is known.

## Additional information

- Research: Another user sees an email from someone's Pipedrive inbox only if three conditions are met: the email thread is set to "Shared", it is linked to a deal or contact, and the user has visibility access to the linked items. — [source](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/email-privacy-and-sharing-emails-in-pipedrive), 2026-10-19
- Research: Each user's default email sharing setting is either "Keep all my email conversations private" (never visible to others) or "Share all my linked email conversations with others in my company"; check the sales manager's setting. — [source](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/email-privacy-and-sharing-emails-in-pipedrive), 2026-10-19
- Research: Shared emails appear only in the detail view of the linked deal, lead, project, contact or organization, not in the other user's mailbox, unless that user took part in the conversation. — [source](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/email-privacy-and-sharing-emails-in-pipedrive), 2026-10-19
- Research: How to view or adjust the visibility of an email in Pipedrive. — [source](https://support.pipedrive.com/article/how-can-i-view-or-adjust-email-visibility), 2026-10-19
