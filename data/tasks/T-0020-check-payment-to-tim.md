## Additional information

- Place: TIM — check payment status in your [MyTIM account](https://mytim.tim.it/it/assistenza-fisso.html) (log in with your email and password; "Problemi di accesso?" recovers the password) or in the MyTIM app, or call 187 (consumer line; 191 is for business customers) to ask about the account balance. — [source](https://selectra.net/internet/guida/fattura/pagamento-bolletta-tim), [source](https://mytim.tim.it/it/assistenza-fisso.html), 2026-10-19
- Research: Payments made in the MyTIM app, on tim.it via P@gonline or through PagoPA reach TIM in real time. If the line has been suspended, a payment already made can be reported in the MyTIM app, which is available 24 hours a day. — [source](https://www.comparasemplice.it/informazioni/tim/bolletta-tim-non-pagata-cosa-succede-e-come-risolvere), 2026-10-19
- Price: A bill paid late can carry a penalty, reported as up to 6% of the amount. Sources differ on when it applies, so check the bill. — [source](https://www.comparasemplice.it/informazioni/tim/bolletta-tim-non-pagata-cosa-succede-e-come-risolvere), 2026-10-19
