## Description

Send the payment receipt (quietanza) for the F24 from T-0017 Pay the overdue F24 for the unpaid 2025 tax to the sender, who urgently needs it. The sender points out that the late-payment amount (ravvedimento operoso) was calculated for payment by 12 October 2026, the date by which the F24 had to be paid; in any case, they absolutely need the receipt. Done when the receipt has been sent.

## Additional information

- Research: When an F24 is paid through home banking or the bank's app, the receipt is available immediately in the banking service. — [source](https://quifinanza.it/tasse/come-pagare-modello-f24-online/284968/), 2026-10-20
