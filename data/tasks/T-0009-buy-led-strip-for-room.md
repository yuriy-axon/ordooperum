## Description

Buy an LED strip for the room, to be mounted in the corner where the ceiling meets the wall. The room is 13 m², so measure the walls first to know how many metres of strip are needed. Done when a strip of the right length, with a power supply and any mounting parts, has been bought.

## Additional information

- Research: A 13 m² room is only about 14–15 m of perimeter if it is roughly square (about 3.6 m × 3.6 m), and more if it is long and narrow. Measure each wall and add a little spare. This is an estimate, not a measurement.
- Research: Corner aluminium profiles with a milky diffuser fit the joint between wall and ceiling, mount on the surface without cutting, hide the LED dots and protect the strip. — [source](https://www.simplelighting.co.uk/products/2m-long-corner-black-aluminium-profile-15-8mm-x-15-8mm), 2026-10-13
- Price: Basic 15 m LED strips on Amazon.it cost about €17–€23 (indicative; depends on brand, RGB or white, and app control). — [source](https://www.telefonino.net/notizie/a-questo-prezzo-su-amazon-la-striscia-led-da-15-metri-e-un-regalo), 2026-10-13
- Price: Philips Hue smart strips (official Italian list prices): Flux 10 m €269.99, Flux 6 m €129.99, Flux 6 m with Hue Bridge Pro starter kit €199.99, OmniGlow 10 m €349.99. — [source](https://www.philips-hue.com/it-it/products/smart-light-strips), 2026-10-13
- Price: A 2 m corner aluminium profile with diffuser costs about €8–€22 each, so about €60–€170 for 15 m (indicative). — [source](https://www.manomano.it/p/profilo-in-alluminio-angolo-coperchio-circolare-2m-per-striscia-led-fino-a-5-mm-transparente-bianco-2-m-85837724), 2026-10-13
