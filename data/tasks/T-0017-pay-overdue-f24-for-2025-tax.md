## Description

Pay the attached F24 form for an unpaid 2025 tax ("tributo insoluto del 2025"), then send the payment receipt (quietanza) back to the sender, who is waiting for it. The email set a firm deadline of 12 October 2026 ("entro e non oltre"), which has already passed, so pay as soon as possible and ask the sender whether the amount has to be updated for the late payment. Done when the F24 is paid and the receipt has been sent to the sender.

## Additional information

- Research: An F24 can be paid through home banking or your bank's app (the receipt is available immediately), or online with the Agenzia delle Entrate's F24 Web service using SPID, CIE or CNS, without fees. — [source](https://quifinanza.it/tasse/come-pagare-modello-f24-online/284968/), 2026-10-18
- Research: A late tax payment can be regularised with "ravvedimento operoso": one F24 with the tax, a reduced penalty and daily legal interest. Paid within 30 days of the deadline, the reduced penalty is 1.25%. Check with the sender whether the attached F24 must be recalculated. — [source](https://centrofiscale.com/ravvedimento-operoso/), 2026-10-18
