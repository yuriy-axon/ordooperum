## Additional information

- Research: In Atlassian Cloud, billing depends on the product plan and the number of users with product access at the end of the billing cycle; billing details are under Administration > Billing. — [source](https://confluence.atlassian.com/cloud/billing-and-user-count-873871345.html), 2026-10-07
- Research: Jira and Confluence Cloud come in Free, Standard, Premium and Enterprise plans; the plan can be changed under Administration > Billing > Manage subscriptions. — [source](https://confluence.atlassian.com/cloud/billing-and-user-count-873871345.html), 2026-10-07
- Research: Atlassian's list prices on a monthly plan are $7.91 per user per month for Jira Standard and $14.54 for Jira Premium, and $5.42 (Standard) or $10.44 (Premium) for Confluence. Both are free for up to 10 users. — [source](https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira/pricing), 2026-10-08
