## Description

Sign the personal tax declaration for 2025 in Italy. To do this, schedule a meeting with the accountant and go to the accountant's office in Milan to sign it. Done when the declaration is signed by the end of October.

## Additional information

- Research: The official deadline for filing the Redditi PF 2026 form (tax year 2025) online is 2 November 2026, because 31 October falls on a Saturday and 1 November is a holiday. Late filing is possible within 90 days (by 1 February 2027), with reduced penalties. — [source](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/modello-redditi-persone-fisiche-2026/quando-e-come-presentare-il-modello-redditi-persone-fisiche), 2026-10-15
