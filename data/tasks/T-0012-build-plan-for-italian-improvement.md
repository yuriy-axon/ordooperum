## Description

Research and agree on a step-by-step plan for improving Italian grammar from B1 towards B2, with 1–2 hours a week. The plan guides the weekly "Learn Italian" task (T-0013). Done when the plan is agreed. Proposed plan:

- Weeks 1–2 (second half of October 2026): take a placement test and list your weak points.
- November to mid-December 2026: consolidate B1 grammar: passato prossimo vs imperfetto, direct, indirect and combined pronouns, ci and ne, futuro and condizionale presente.
- Mid-December 2026 to February 2027: congiuntivo presente and passato, and when to use them (credo che, è importante che, benché, prima che).
- March to April 2027: congiuntivo imperfetto and trapassato, concordanza dei tempi, and the three types of periodo ipotetico, plus mixed ones.
- May to June 2027: discorso indiretto, the passive and si passivante, gerundio and participio, and connectives that require the congiuntivo (affinché, nonostante, qualora).
- July to October 2027: mixed review and writing practice; optionally a CILS B2 mock test.
- Weekly session: 20 minutes on the rule, 30 minutes of exercises, 20 minutes writing a short text that uses it, 10 minutes reviewing your mistakes log.

## Additional information

- Project: Current level B1; 1–2 hours per week for grammar (from data/projects/personal/italia.md).
- Research: At B2 you are expected to master all three types of periodo ipotetico and mixed conditionals, use qualora, ove and nel caso in cui with the congiuntivo, and shift tenses correctly in reported speech. — [source](https://fastitalianlearning.com/learn/b2/periodo-ipotetico-b2), 2026-10-16
- Research: CILS (Università per Stranieri di Siena) is the official certification; B2 is "CILS DUE-B2". In 2026 the B2 sessions were on 14 April, 16 June and 3 December. Check the 2027 dates before planning a test. — [source](https://www.aisphila.org/cils-certification), 2026-10-16
