## Additional information

- Research: For a fair test of two outreach versions, keep one as the control, send each to at least about 250 contacts, run for at least two weeks and don't stop early because one looks better. Reply-rate differences need far bigger samples than open-rate differences (at a 1.5% reply rate, about 4,400 contacts per version to detect a +50% lift). — [source](https://www.woodpecker.co/cold-email-ab-test-calculator/), 2026-10-26
