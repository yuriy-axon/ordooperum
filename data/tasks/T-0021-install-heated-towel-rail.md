## Description

Install a heated towel rail in the apartment. First decide between an electric model and one connected to the heating system, then buy it and have it installed. Done when the towel rail is mounted and working.

## Additional information

- Price: Electric towel rails sold in Italy cost roughly 120–180 EUR, for example the Fabrilamp SCUTI from 123.45 EUR at MediaWorld. Prices are indicative and depend on size, power (about 300–750 W) and features such as a thermostat or timer. — [source](https://www.mediaworld.it/it/product/_radiatore-fabrilamp-scuti-184250704.html), 2026-10-20
- Research: An electric towel rail needs no plumbing work: it can use a plug or be wired into the electrical system with an on/off switch. It must be kept at a safe distance from water; a qualified electrician can tell you where it may go. — [source](https://www.arredamento.it/scaldasalviette-elettrico.asp), 2026-10-20
