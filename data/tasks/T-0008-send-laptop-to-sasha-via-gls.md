## Description

Ship a laptop to Sasha in the Czech Republic using GLS, sending it from Gravellona Toce. This is critical and should be done as soon as possible. Done when the parcel has been handed over to GLS and the tracking number has been shared with Sasha.

## Additional information

- Research: The GLS branch in Gravellona Toce ("GLS Sede di Verbania") is at Via Nuova 148, 28883 Gravellona Toce (VB); phone 0323 865535 or toll-free 800 184726. — [source](https://www.paginegialle.it/gravellona-toce-vb/corrieri/gls_T11443343), 2026-10-08
- Research: Opening hours listed for the branch: Monday–Friday 08:30–13:00 and 14:30–18:30, Saturday 08:30–12:00, closed on Sunday. These come from a business directory, not from GLS, so call ahead to confirm. — [source](https://www.paginegialle.it/gravellona-toce-vb/corrieri/gls_T11443343), 2026-10-08
- Research: GLS publishes no fixed price list for private senders; the branch quotes the price. As a guide, standard courier shipping from Italy to the Czech Republic costs about €23.90 for 5 kg (€26.90 for 10 kg) with delivery in 3–4 working days, or about €55 for 24-hour express (online reseller rates, not GLS's own). — [source](https://www.spediresubito.com/spedire-in-repubblica-ceca), 2026-10-08
- Research: GLS has a dedicated guide on safely transporting lithium batteries, which applies to laptops; mention the battery when booking. — [source](https://gls-group.com/IT/en/send-receive/how-to-ship/), 2026-10-08
