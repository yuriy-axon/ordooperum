## Goal

Agree on how we present our services to potential clients, decide what the data set covers first, and align on tools, buying signals and the contact form changes.

## Agenda

### 1. How we present our services to potential clients — P1 · 12 min

The main topic: how our services are presented to potential clients, and what should change.

- **Key points:** Agree on the core message and which services we lead with. Decide who updates the presentation and by when.
- **Supporting material:**
  - Check: the current presentation material and how potential clients receive it.
- **Likely questions:** "What exactly is wrong with the current presentation?" — bring two or three concrete examples. "Who is the target client?" — agree on this before discussing wording.
- **Outcome wanted:** An agreed direction for the service presentation, with an owner and a deadline.

### 2. Data set: what the program covers first — P2 · 6 min

Decide which data the program starts with and what comes later.

- **Key points:** Agree on the first scope of the data set. Name what is explicitly out of scope for now.
- **Supporting material:**
  - Check: the current state of the data set and what is already available.
- **Likely questions:** "Where does the data come from, and who maintains it?" — check before the meeting.
- **Outcome wanted:** An agreed first scope for the data set.

### 3. Tools: what needs to be done based on what clients need protected — P2 · 5 min

The usual review of our tools: what needs to be done, driven by what clients need to protect.

- **Key points:** Start from client needs, not from the tools. List the open tool work and who owns it.
- **Supporting material:**
  - Check: the open tool work and the client requirements behind it.
- **Likely questions:** "Which client need is most urgent?" — rank them before the meeting.
- **Outcome wanted:** A short, prioritized list of tool work with owners.

### 4. Contact form edits and changes in progress — P2 · 4 min

Review the planned edits to the contact form and the changes that are already being made.

- **Key points:** Confirm which edits are planned and which are already running. Keep the form short; ask for only what sales needs for the first contact.
- **Supporting material:**
  - Research: The optimal number of fields for a B2B lead form is 3–5; each extra field lowers conversion by about 4% on average, and a required phone field is the single biggest drop-off. — [source](https://brixongroup.com/en/lead-forms-in-b2b-the-perfect-balancing-act-between-data-depth-and-conversion-rate), 2026-10-13
  - Check: the current contact form fields and the status of the changes in progress.
- **Likely questions:** "When will the current changes be live?" — check the status first.
- **Outcome wanted:** An agreed list of contact form edits and a date for the changes in progress.

### 5. Buying signals — P3 · 3 min

Agree on which buying signals we use to spot potential clients who are ready to talk, and how sales acts on them.

- **Key points:** Agree on the few signals we track first. Decide who watches them and how quickly sales follows up.
- **Supporting material:**
  - Research: Buying signals are explicit (a demo request, a contact form, a reply to outreach) or implicit (repeat website visits, returns to pricing or service pages, guide downloads, webinar attendance). First-party signals from our own website and campaigns are more reliable than third-party intent data. — [source](https://bitscale.ai/blogs/what-are-buying-signals-in-b2b-sales-types-examples-and-how-to-use-them), 2026-10-13
  - Check: which signals we can already see today (website analytics, contact form, email replies).
- **Likely questions:** "Which signal means a lead is ready for a call?" — propose one or two to start with.
- **Outcome wanted:** A short list of buying signals to track and an owner for following them up.

## Preparation

- Bring two or three examples of how we currently present our services.
- Check the current state of the data set.
- List the open tool work and the client needs behind it.
- Check the current contact form fields and the status of the changes in progress.
- Check which buying signals we can already see (website analytics, contact form, email replies).
